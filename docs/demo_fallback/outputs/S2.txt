🛂 Obtaining a passport for the first time or renewing it for Tunisians residing in Tunisia.

▪️ Required documents
• A machine-readable passport application form, to be filled out and signed in the designated area.
• Copy of the National Identity Card, with presentation of the original or a birth certificate for minors.
• 4 photos with the following characteristics:
• white background
• 3.5/4.5 cm format
• Proof of enrollment for students and learners.
• Authorization from the guardian for minors, accompanied by a copy of their National Identity Card.
• Receipt for payment of the applicable tax stamp fee:
• 20 dinars for students, learners and children under 6 years of age,
• 60 dinars for others.
• Attach the old passport if renewing,
• Submit a standard paper application in case the applicant wishes to keep the old passport.

▪️ Conditions
• The applicant must:
• Be of Tunisian nationality,
• Be free from the restrictions indicated by Law No. 75-40 of 14/05/1975 relating to passports and travel documents and subsequent texts.

▪️ Where to submit
• Local police or National Guard post

▪️ Processing time
• Within 15 days from the date of submission of the application.

⚠️ Warning: the official FR and AR versions of this page state different amounts (20/60 vs 25/80). Confirm the current amount with the administration.
